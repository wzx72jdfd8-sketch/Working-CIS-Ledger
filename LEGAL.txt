CIS LEDGER — LEGAL NOTES
========================

Product: CIS Ledger
Version: 1.1.4 (1 September 2026)

Not HMRC software
-----------------
CIS Ledger is not HMRC-recognised tax software. It does not file CIS300
monthly returns. It does not file Self Assessment. It does not connect
to HMRC or verify subcontractors. It does not replace the contractor's
official payment and deduction statement.

Records only
------------
The app produces invoices and records you keep on this computer. It is
a record-keeping aid, not tax advice.

Your responsibility
-------------------
You are responsible for the accuracy of names, UTRs, rates, line types,
VAT, and amounts. Check figures against the contractor's statement and
take advice from HMRC or a qualified accountant where you need it.
CIS rates are set when the contractor verifies you with HMRC.

Local data only
---------------
Your profile, contractors, invoices, next invoice number and unlock PIN
live in cis-ledger.json in the CIS Ledger folder (the same place as the
starter). Data is not uploaded. Clearing the browser does not wipe the
books. Deleting that JSON file does. Keep a JSON backup from Settings.
The unlock PIN is not encryption. Anyone with this computer can open
the file.
